- Benefit: distinguish proven-unparseable remote state from an ordinary cache miss so periodic top-up does not create deterministic retry/parse/log churn.

- Follow-up: preserve as an adopted reconciliation invariant. No port branch is needed unless future refactoring removes or replaces this mechanism.
