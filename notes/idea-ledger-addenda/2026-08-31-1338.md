### Deduplication note

This is not the same as general domain-specific dirty tracking or restore safety. It is a narrower destructive-write invariant: **only explicit delete authority (or a separately proven complete replacement transaction) may remove persisted entities; omission from a partial manifest is non-destructive.** Preserve related storage-architecture items separately and merge future source evidence into this Feature-ID rather than creating duplicate tombstone entries.
