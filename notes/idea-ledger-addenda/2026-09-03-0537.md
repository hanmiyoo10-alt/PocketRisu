### Why it matters

Streaming UI reparses can happen much faster than an external translation call completes. Treating each reparse as fresh authority creates request amplification and unstable UI state. The durable rule is to serialize expensive translation work per logical target, coalesce obsolete intermediate snapshots, and publish only renderable current results while keeping the last good output as fallback.
