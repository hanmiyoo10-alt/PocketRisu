- benefit: keeps optimistic plugin-storage reads responsive while preventing failed/stale writes from fabricating local success or undoing a newer server-committed value
- conflict/risk: value-based rollback ownership is ambiguous for overlapping equal-value writes; stale rollback can corrupt local view after a newer success; clear/reset can be undone by in-flight failures unless rollback authority is invalidated
- validation need: rejected set/remove, overlapping same-key equal/unequal writes, clear during an in-flight failure, and sidecar metadata failure after durable success
- follow-up: preserve as an adopted concurrency invariant and reuse the per-operation generation/token pattern when future server-backed optimistic caches need failure rollback

Dedup note: distinct from `PLUGIN-STORAGE-REFRESH-TOP-UP-ONLY`; that item governs bounded refresh/read ownership, while this one governs optimistic mutation rollback authority under concurrency.
