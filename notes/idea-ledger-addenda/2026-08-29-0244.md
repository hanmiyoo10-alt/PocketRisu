### Design invariant

A chat duplicate is a new durable object created from a complete source snapshot, not a structured clone of whatever subset is presently materialized in UI/runtime memory. Any async hydration step must revalidate stable source identity before commit, and any source-internal identifiers or references that must be unique in the duplicate must be remapped together.

### Relationship to existing ideas

Cross-link with `STORAGE-DEFERRED-DOMAIN-COMPLETE-SNAPSHOT`: both reject shallow runtime projections as authoritative complete state. Keep them distinct because export/backup completeness and object-clone identity semantics have different ownership, validation, and rollback boundaries.
