### Durable invariant

Temporary loading UI is not authoritative content. It may temporarily replace the rendered slot only under an explicitly eligible request/runtime state, and when the async flight ends unsuccessfully it must restore the best valid rendered result or the pre-flight fallback. Streaming anti-flicker policy is narrower than manual/non-streaming loading policy.

### Dedupe

Keep separate from generic stale-flight cancellation, translation-cache behavior, and broad spinner/flicker polish. This record owns the state-transition rule that a failed async translation cannot leave a temporary loading sentinel as durable rendered content.
