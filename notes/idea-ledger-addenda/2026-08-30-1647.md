### Deduplication / history

This is not a new patch-architecture proposal. It refines the existing adopted save/patch safety history with a concrete bounded-array invariant. It is complementary to snapshot/patch-failure safety: snapshot correctness protects rollback, while this invariant prevents patch construction itself from failing before a request can be made.
