- Importance: `HIGH`
- Difficulty: `LOW`
- Size: `XS`
- Evidence: `HIGH`

- Lifecycle: `ADOPTED`
- Source evidence: `PocketRisu/PocketRisu` `2c6cc72413e9a48a28bb625d39d38975b105993d`
- Benefit: prevents terminal job recovery from racing a live send that is replaying/reattaching after tab return, avoiding duplicate/raw message fill, redundant save, duplicate claim, and duplicate request-log effects.
- Conflict/risk: an over-broad or stale ownership guard could delay legitimate recovery; the gate must only honor current `live` ownership and must allow later recovery once that owner releases.

- Follow-up: preserve owner-kind semantics anywhere terminal/background recovery is refactored; do not replace with a blanket "generation exists" check.
