#### Benefit

A recovery attempt must not claim that data is absent from every backup when it actually examined only a subset. The source fix carries explicit counts for existing, checked, unreadable, and budget-skipped candidates, and only allows a definitive "not found" conclusion after exhaustive coverage. It also prevents one oversized candidate from starving smaller affordable candidates and keeps the whole repair scan bounded by a wall-clock budget.

#### Conflict / risk

Recovery is a persistent/high-blast-radius area. Candidate budgets, timeouts, ordering, compressed/decompressed limits, and worker-memory ceilings are device/data dependent. Copying RisuVault constants or widening search budgets blindly could stall PocketRisu startup or increase memory pressure. This evidence does not authorize destructive recovery changes.

#### Validation need

- prove whether PocketRisu currently has an equivalent candidate-based repair/recovery path;
- distinguish `all candidates checked and absent` from `some candidates unreadable/skipped/timed out`;
- report census/provenance without exposing sensitive paths;
- test zero-backup, all-unreadable, one-hit, skipped-large-before-small-affordable, timeout, corrupt candidate, and exhaustive-no-hit cases;
- verify failure never publishes partial state;
- measure candidate count/bytes/time/RSS before choosing any limits.
